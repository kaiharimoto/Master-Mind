**M1 · artifact 04 · renderer.** The new selection chip occludes three of the nineteen search hits the frame is demonstrating, dropping them from 0.62 relative luminance to 0.19/0.23/0.27 — from "lit hit" to near-background, which is the avoid-list's "states stop reading at a glance" in reverse. **Fix:** place the chip with the same collision solver the label arbiter now uses (it demonstrably works — see the leader ticks in 02), or offset it to empty canvas; add the `everyNodeUnoccludedByChrome` check that artifact 12 already has to 04, 08 and 10.

**M2 · evidence · DIFF/MANIFEST.** The recipe fingerprint does not cover the claim predicates, and the claim diff is name-only. Artifacts 02 and 06 each *gained* a machine-checked claim while their `fnSha` stayed byte-identical (`73b7deb0e7bd2172`, `c3c27bb00aca3d71`); only the app bundle changed (`3e825182…` → `e6ed98b7…`). So `recipeFingerprintChanged: false` is not evidence that an artifact was produced or judged the same way, and a claim *weakened* rather than renamed would leave no trace at all — I could only confirm that no shared claim's `expected/actual/pass` triple changed, which for predicates that report bare `true` proves little. The removal path DIFF advertises is also untested this cycle. **Fix:** fingerprint each claim predicate (a per-claim `fnSha`) alongside the capture recipe, and diff those hashes, not just the names.

**M3 · artifacts 08, 12 · ui.** The "N thoughts on screen without room for a label" panel counts the selected node even when its selection chip is displaying that exact text, making the on-frame count wrong by one (08 "After": 4 stated, 3 actual; 12 top row: 2 stated, 1 actual).
